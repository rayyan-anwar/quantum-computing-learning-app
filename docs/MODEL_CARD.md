# System Card

Author: Rayyan Anwar

**System:** a deterministic quantum circuit simulator with a teaching interface. No machine-learning model or external data is involved.

**Intended use:** learning how gates, superposition, interference, entanglement and measurement work, with step-by-step state inspection. **Not for:** claims about real quantum hardware, benchmarking quantum devices, cryptography or any production use.

**Content accuracy:** explanations are brief simplifications written for this project; check textbooks for rigorous treatments. Examples (Bell, GHZ, Grover, teleportation) are verified numerically against analytic results.

**Limitations:** at most 6 qubits and 60 gates; ideal noise-free simulation; teleportation shown with deferred measurement (no mid-circuit measurement or classical registers); no custom gates or multi-controlled gates; Bloch spheres show reduced single-qubit states (mixed states appear as shorter vectors).
